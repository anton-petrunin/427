Grading policy:

The two lowest HWA and quiz scores will be dropped; no makeups.

The total score is the highest of the following three percentages:

  • X = your total homework and quizzes percentage, capped at 70%.
  • Y = your percentage on midterm exams capped at 80%, plus X/10;
  • Z = your final (oral) exam percentage, plus MAX{ X/10, Y/10 }.
In addition, each successfully completed extra-credit problem adds 4% to the total course score.
  • 90% guarantees "A".
  • 80% guarantees "B".
  • 70% guarantees "C".
  • 60% guarantees "D".
The lowest 3% points of a range will be minus and the highest 3% points of a range will be plus.

HWA/Quizzes/Midterms/Final/Extra

  • HWA: usually assigned on Wed and usually is due next Mon.
  • Quizzes: once a week, usually on Wed; it is always based on previous HWA.
  • Midterm exams:
    • 1st midterm: Fri Oct 16 (subject to change), in class.

  • Final oral exam: TBD (Dec 14--18) You will have one hour to prepare a mini-presentation on one random topic (ticket). The examiner may interrupt your presentation and ask additional questions. If your answer is satisfactory, you will be given an additional problem for a perfect score.
  • Extra credit problems: The solutions should be presented orally. Only the first solution will be graded. The solutions will NOT be graded after Fri, Nov 20.
